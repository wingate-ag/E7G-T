## 1. What E7G-T Is

E7G-T is a geometry-first modelling language and practical calculus for making **projection, loss, reconstruction, bridge mode, and admissible use** visible.

It is designed to help people inspect representations before relying on them.

Typical representations include:

- AI answers;
- business dashboards;
- charts and metrics;
- translations;
- proof-assistant sessions;
- legal or administrative summaries;
- scientific models;
- speculative bridges;
- software traces;
- reports and decision documents.

---

## 2. What E7G-T Is Not

E7G-T is not established mathematics.

It is not empirical physics.

It is not a proof system.

It is not a Theory of Everything.

It does not replace mature formal, empirical, legal, medical, engineering, translation, statistical, or domain-specific methods.

Its proper role is to frame, inspect, document, teach, bridge, lint, and discipline reasoning around representations.

## 6. Core Operating Question

For any object, answer, chart, proof, translation, model, or claim, ask:

> What do I do next with this concrete representation?

The E7G-T answer requires identifying:

- entity;
- order profile;
- temporal regime;
- context;
- boundary;
- operation;
- preservation/loss;
- reconstruction status;
- bridge mode;
- admissible use;
- stop condition.

## 10. Reviewer Warning

Do not evaluate E7G-T as if it claims to be finished mathematics or physics.

Evaluate it as:

- a modelling language;
- a reasoning discipline;
- a projection-loss accounting tool;
- a claim-strength linter;
- a practical bridge-control method;
- an early-stage proof/path documentation concept.

The correct question is not:

> Has E7G-T solved reality?

The correct question is:

> Does E7G-T help users avoid overreading representations and make better next moves?

# Changelog

## v0.10-UC1 — 2026-07-18

### Added

- unified constitutional core;
- explicit separation of semantic context and inquiry;
- inquiry-relative operational phase semantics;
- phase criteria, phase equivalence, and material phase boundaries;
- typed-change and transition discipline;
- representative-dependence and operation-order checks;
- combined projection-to-phase calculus;
- reconstruction-fibre phase-candidate rule;
- distinct model, admissibility, phase, and bridge boundaries;
- modular E7-Line, E7-Check, E7-Analysis, and FormalisationCard profiles;
- migration guide and unified pilot materials.

### Changed

- D0–D7 are optional modelling-role lenses rather than substitutes for semantic context or phase semantics;
- viewing is the general representation operation, with order projection as a specialised subtype;
- transformation invariants are distinguished from phase-defining invariants;
- temporal declarations are separated from motifs derived from transition structure;
- the public definition now includes operational sameness, material boundaries, and transition paths.

### Deprecated

- v0.9 RC1 as the active kernel;
- root-level links presenting the old RC1 kernel as current;
- use of one undifferentiated “boundary” field in consequential analyses;
- phase inference directly from a lossy view without reconstruction-fibre analysis.
